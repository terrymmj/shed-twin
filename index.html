How to use it

Overview
Drag to orbit, scroll or pinch to zoom, right-drag or two fingers to pan. The roof starts hidden so you can see in; R brings it back.
Plan
Top-down floor plan with a 4 ft grid. Drag to pan, scroll to zoom.
Walk
Stand in the shed at eye height. Drag to look, WASD or arrow keys to move, on-screen arrows on touch screens. Click the minimap to jump.
Photo
The model seen from where you took the photo, with your photo laid over it. Slide the photo in and out to see where the model is off.
Items
Click anything in the model, a label, or a row in the list. Search matches names and tags like EQ-02.
Accuracy
Colors every item by how its position is known: fitted to the photo's perspective, estimated from the photo, assumed because it's out of frame, or measured.
Measure
Click two points on any surface to read the distance in the model.
Keys
1–4 views · / search · F focus selected · Esc close or stop measuring.
